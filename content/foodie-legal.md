## 8. Allergen and dietary disclaimer

Allergen, ingredient, and dietary information (such as "halal," "vegetarian," "gluten-free," or "contains peanuts") on the Site comes from businesses or public sources and **may be incomplete or out of date**. We do not test food or verify these claims.

Many Filipino dishes contain common allergens that may not be obvious from the dish name — for example, shrimp paste (bagoong), fish sauce (patis), peanuts (such as in kare-kare), crustaceans and shellfish, eggs, soy, wheat, and milk. Recipes vary between kitchens, and cross-contamination can happen.

**If you have a food allergy, intolerance, or dietary or religious requirement, always confirm ingredients and preparation directly with the business before ordering or eating.** In an emergency, call 911.

## 9. Health and food safety

We are not a public health authority and do not inspect restaurants, caterers, home-based food businesses, or grocery stores. Being listed, verified, featured, or highly scored on the Site is **not** a statement that a business meets food-safety, licensing, or health requirements.

Food-safety inspections in Ontario are carried out by local public health units. You can check inspection results through your local public health unit (for example, DineSafe in Toronto) where available. Some traditional delicacies (such as balut, raw or fermented seafood, or street-style foods) may carry higher food-safety risks for some people, including pregnant people, young children, older adults, and people with weakened immune systems. Use your own judgment and consult a health professional if you have concerns.
