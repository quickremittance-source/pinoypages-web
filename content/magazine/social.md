## Post 2: HST $30,000 rule (piece 3)

**EN**
Side hustle growing? 🍢🧹💻 Quick question many of us get: **"Do I need to charge HST?"**
In short: once your **taxable sales go over $30,000** (in one quarter, or over four quarters in a row), you're generally no longer a "small supplier" and must register. It counts **sales before expenses**, not profit.
We explained it with a simple example, plus a free checker that gives you a general indication: [link]
_General info only, not tax advice. Rideshare drivers have different rules._

**TL**
Lumalaki na ba ang sideline mo? 🍢🧹💻 Madalas itanong: **"Kailangan ko na bang mag-charge ng HST?"**
Sa madaling salita: kapag ang **taxable sales mo ay lumampas sa $30,000** (sa isang quarter, o sa apat na magkakasunod na quarter), karaniwang hindi ka na "small supplier" at kailangan mo nang mag-register. **Sales bago ibawas ang gastos** ang binibilang, hindi profit.
Ipinaliwanag namin ito gamit ang simpleng halimbawa, kasama ang libreng checker na nagbibigay ng pangkalahatang indikasyon: [link]

## Post 3: CPP for self-employed (piece 4)

**EN**
Went independent this year? Caregiver, nurse, contractor, freelancer? 👋
Heads-up: when you're self-employed you pay **both halves of CPP**. For 2026 that's **11.9%** of net self-employment income between $3,500 and $74,600, plus **8%** between $74,600 and $85,000 (official CRA/ESDC figures).
We made a plain-language guide with worked examples, plus a free estimator: [link]
Budget for it now so April 30 isn't a shock 🙏
_General info only, not tax advice._

Nag-independent ka ba ngayong taon? Caregiver, nurse, contractor, freelancer? 👋
Paalala: kapag self-employed ka, **ikaw ang magbabayad ng parehong bahagi ng CPP**. Para sa 2026, **11.9%** ito ng net self-employment income mula $3,500 hanggang $74,600, at **8%** mula $74,600 hanggang $85,000 (opisyal na figures ng CRA/ESDC).

## Post 5: Benefits families miss (piece 6)

**EN**
"Wala naman akong income, kailangan ko pa bang mag-file?" 🤔
Often, **yes**. Payments like the **Canada Child Benefit**, the **Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit** (that's the new name of the GST/HST credit since July 2026) and the **Ontario Trillium Benefit** are based on your tax return. Both spouses need to file.
Renters: rent can count toward the Ontario Energy and Property Tax Credit. Don't skip the ON-BEN form!
Official 2026–27 amounts and a checklist: [link]
_General info only, not tax advice._

**TL**
"Wala naman akong income, kailangan ko pa bang mag-file?" 🤔
Kadalasan, **oo**. Ang mga bayad gaya ng **Canada Child Benefit**, **Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit** (bagong pangalan ng GST/HST credit simula July 2026) at **Ontario Trillium Benefit** ay base sa tax return mo. Kailangang mag-file ang mag-asawa.
Sa mga umuupa: puwedeng mabilang ang renta sa Ontario Energy and Property Tax Credit. Huwag laktawan ang ON-BEN form!
