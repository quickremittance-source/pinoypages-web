# Filipino Heritage Month in Canada (June)

_From Pinoy Pages 2026–2027_

**Filipino Heritage Month** is observed in Canada throughout **June**. It is a time to celebrate Filipino culture and the contributions of Filipino Canadians — and it sits beside **Philippine Independence Day on June 12**.

## Official recognition

On **1 June 2026**, the Honourable Marc Miller, Minister of Canadian Identity and Culture and Minister responsible for Official Languages, issued a Government of Canada statement marking Filipino Heritage Month. The statement invited everyone to discover Filipino traditions, stories and values throughout June, and wished those celebrating Philippine Independence Day on **12 June** a happy **128th** anniversary.

The statement also noted that, with nearly **one million** people, the Filipino-Canadian community is among the fastest growing in Canada, with contributions across health, education, the arts, sport, science, entrepreneurship, finance, social justice and public life.

**Source:** [Statement by Minister Miller on Filipino Heritage Month](https://www.canada.ca/en/canadian-heritage/news/2026/05/statement-by-minister-miller-on-filipino-heritage-month.html) (Canadian Heritage). See also Canadian Heritage’s list of [important and commemorative days](https://www.canada.ca/en/canadian-heritage/services/important-commemorative-days.html).

Earlier ministerial statements marked the month in prior years (for example 2024 and 2025 statements on canada.ca), showing an ongoing federal practice of commemorating June as Filipino Heritage Month.

## How communities usually mark June

Federal statements do not prescribe a single festival. In practice, kabayan often see:

- Philippine Independence Day flag-raisings and receptions hosted with local governments and the [Philippine Embassy in Ottawa](https://ottawape.dfa.gov.ph/) or Consulates
- Cultural shows, food fairs, Masses of thanksgiving, and sports tournaments organized by regional associations
- Library and museum exhibits, and school activities

Pinoy Pages will list verified June events on the [events calendar](/calendar) when organizers submit them. Always confirm date, place and tickets with the organizer.
