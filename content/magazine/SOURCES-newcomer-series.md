## Topics where sources were thin / caveats

1. **Christmas balikbayan cut-off dates** — No single BOC national “ship by X for Christmas” date found. Articles state forwarder-set cut-offs only; do not invent dates. Direct `customs.gov.ph` fetches returned **403** from this environment; CAO text used via Supreme Court E-Library.
2. **Simbang Gabi in Canada** — Strong sourcing for the Philippine tradition (Catholics & Cultures). No federal/provincial official Mass calendar; Canadian practice is parish-by-parish. No invented parish schedules.
3. **Canadian phone number** — No dedicated Service Canada “get a phone” program page; section is practical orientation only (compare carriers, ID, keep number stable).
4. **Caregiver PR pathways** — All major caregiver **PR** pilots shown **Closed** or intake **paused** (IRCC Dec 19, 2025 notice: no March 2026 reopen). Article correctly emphasizes temporary LMIA work as the still-described option and warns not to invent reopen dates.
5. **BSP remittance rules** — FCAC + Bank of Canada used for Canada-side comparison. No provider rankings. BSP-specific OFW remittance product rules were not required for the compare-fees brief and were not used as rankings.
6. **Tagalog** — All TL files need **native-speaker review** before publish (same rule as existing magazine TL).
7. **Piece 10 vs repo 05/06** — Overlap intentional; coordinate with Coder before merging into `content/magazine` / `guide.json` to avoid duplicate slugs.
