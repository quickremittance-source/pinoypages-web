# The tax preparer who keeps tissues on the desk in April

Every April, a box of tissues appears on Maria Santos's desk. It sits beside the stapler, within easy reach of whoever takes the chair across from her, and by the end of the month she has usually replaced it twice.

"People think I'm joking," she says. "I'm not. Somebody opens a letter from the CRA they've been carrying in their purse since January, and they've convinced themselves it's the end of the world. Most of the time it's a missing slip."

Santos runs [Bituin Tax & Bookkeeping](/listing/tax-01) from a modest unit in a plaza near Malvern, in Scarborough. She began in 2014 at her dining table on Sheppard Avenue, preparing returns for nurses in her apartment building who were receiving correspondence they could neither decipher nor ignore. Several of those nurses are still clients. Some now send their adult children.

Her practice is deliberately narrow: personal returns, self-employment income, and the bewildering paperwork that follows a reassessment. She declines corporate work. "I would rather do one thing properly," she says, "than six things in a hurry."

What distinguishes her, according to the people who refer others to her, is an almost stubborn insistence on explanation. A client does not leave until Santos has walked through the notice of assessment line by line, in English or Tagalog, and the client can repeat back what it says. She keeps a binder of anonymised sample notices for exactly that purpose.

Self-employed clients receive a particular lecture about the Canada Pension Plan, which catches many first-year contractors off guard because they owe both the employee and employer portions. She sends them to the [CPP estimator](/tools/cpp-estimator) before they arrive, so the figure is not a surprise across the desk.

In November 2026 Santos became one of the first businesses in the directory to earn the Verified Kabayan badge. The visit took about fifteen minutes. Her vouching partners are [Hiraya Insurance & Mortgage](/listing/ins-01) in Etobicoke and [Perlas Bookkeeping & Payroll](/listing/biz-01) in Markham, two practices she has exchanged referrals with for years.

She is unsentimental about why she signed up. "My clients are careful people. They should be able to check that I'm who I say I am before they hand me their SIN." Her ZCard, reached through the QR code taped to her front window, lets them save her number without typing it.

She will be running a [self-employed tax workshop](/calendar/ev-sample-featured) in late February, in English and Tagalog. Bring receipts, she says, and bring the envelope you have been avoiding.
