# Kabayan's Pick, and the case for letting members decide

Most business awards are decided by a panel, and there are good reasons for that. Panels can weigh evidence, compare like with like, and resist the temptation to reward whoever has the largest group chat.

Pinoy Pages will have a panel too. When the awards launch alongside the [April 2027 edition](/awards), a small group drawn from Verified members will select Business of the Year and the best business in each sub-category of the directory. A separate jury will choose Ad of the Year from the advertisements that appear in the edition itself.

One award, however, will belong entirely to readers. Kabayan's Pick is decided by member vote, and the reasoning behind it is straightforward. The people best placed to say which bookkeeper returns calls, which insurance advisor explained a policy patiently, or which tax preparer stayed late in April, are the people who were on the other side of the desk.

The mechanics are intentionally plain. Members will be able to vote once per email address while voting is open. Only businesses with an active Verified Kabayan badge will be eligible, which means every nominee will have passed an in-person visit and carry a mutual vouch from another verified business. Votes will be counted after voting closes, and the result announced without ranking the runners-up, because a public league table of small businesses serves nobody well.

There will be no paid nominations and no premium entries. A business cannot purchase a place on the ballot, in the same way it cannot purchase the badge.

Ramon dela Cruz of [Dagat Tax Services](/listing/tax-04) in Mississauga, whose client list is weighted heavily toward recent arrivals, welcomed the format. "Newcomers don't write online reviews," he says. "They tell their cousin. A vote is closer to how our community actually recommends people."

Cristina Navarro of [Tanglaw Career Coaching](/listing/ind-05) was more circumspect. "Any vote rewards the people with the biggest networks," she notes. "That's why I'm glad the category awards are judged separately."

Both points are fair, and the design attempts to honour each. The rules, dates and voting details will be published with the edition. Nothing is open yet, and there is nothing to sign up for.

The [awards launch evening](/calendar/ev-awards-apr) is planned for April 16, 2027, in Toronto. Businesses that would like to be eligible can begin with [Get Verified](/get-verified).
