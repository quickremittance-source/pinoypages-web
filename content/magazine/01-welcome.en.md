# Welcome to Pinoy Pages 2026–2027: The Filipino Business Directory (Category: Money & Business)

Mabuhay! **Pinoy Pages 2026–2027** is the annual directory of Filipino-Canadian money and business services in the Greater Toronto Area. This edition covers **Spring, Summer and Fall** and is released on **April 16, 2027**, and the directory is online all year at pinoypages.ca. It covers the topic our kababayan ask about most: **money**. That means tax preparers, bookkeepers and accountants, insurance advisors, banking and mortgage professionals, and the independent pros and small businesses our community relies on.

## Who it's for

- **Filipino families in the GTA**, whether you've been here 30 years or 30 days
- **Newcomers**, including families with an OFW background who are learning how things work in Canada
- **Self-employed Pinoys and small business owners**: caregivers who went independent, contractors, freelancers, home-based sellers, and side hustlers

## What you'll find here

- **A directory** of Filipino-Canadian money and business services (tax, accounting, insurance, banking/mortgage, independent pros and small businesses). Every listing shows the business name and contact details, so you can reach them yourself.
- **Free tools:**
  - **HST registration checker**: answer a few questions to see whether you might be past the $30,000 small supplier limit → [HST checker](https://pinoypages.ca/tools/hst-checker) _[URL TBC]_
  - **Self-employed CPP estimator**: a rough idea of your CPP contributions on self-employment income → [CPP estimator](https://pinoypages.ca/tools/cpp-estimator) _[URL TBC]_
  - **Tax deadlines page**: the key dates for 2027 tax season in one place → [Tax deadlines](https://pinoypages.ca/tax-deadlines) _[URL TBC]_
- **Plain-language guides** to Canadian tax and benefits, in English and Tagalog

## How we work

- **Listings are free.** Any qualifying business can get a basic listing (name and contact) at no cost. Businesses can also pay to upgrade, and paid listings are always labelled.
- **We don't recommend or rate businesses.** Being listed is not an endorsement. Do your own checks before you hire anyone (our guide on [choosing a tax preparer](/guides/choose-tax-preparer) can help).
- **We publish one edition a year** (this one is Pinoy Pages 2026–2027), and the website is updated all year.

Salamat for being here. If you have a question you'd like us to cover, tell us. That's how we decide what to write next.
