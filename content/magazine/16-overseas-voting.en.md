# Overseas voting (OAV) for Filipinos in Canada

_From Pinoy Pages 2026–2027_

Filipino citizens living in Canada can register and vote in Philippine national elections through **Overseas Absentee Voting** (also called overseas voting), administered with the Commission on Elections (**COMELEC**) Office for Overseas Voting and Philippine foreign posts under the Department of Foreign Affairs (**DFA**).

This summary follows notices from Philippine Consulates in Canada and COMELEC references they cite. **Confirm every appointment and document list with your post** before you travel.

## Registration period for the 2028 elections

Philippine Consulate General notices (for example Vancouver and Calgary) state that overseas voter registration for the **2028** Philippine elections runs from:

**1 December 2025 to 30 September 2027**

Legal references cited by posts include **Republic Act No. 9189**, as amended by **RA 10590**, and **COMELEC Resolution No. 11171** (27 August 2025).

**Who may register (as stated by posts):**
- A citizen of the Philippines, not otherwise disqualified by law
- At least **18 years old on or before 8 May 2028**

**Source:** [Vancouver PCG notice](https://www.vancouverpcg.org/notice-of-resumption-of-overseas-voter-registration-from-01-december-2025-to-30-september-2027/), [Calgary PCG — Overseas voting](https://calgarypcg.ca/voting-philippines/)

## Where to file

Applications may be filed:

- At any **Philippine Embassy or Consulate** (or other designated post abroad)
- At designated registration centres announced by the post
- Through COMELEC Office for Overseas Voting / designated centres in the Philippines (as applicable)

In Canada, check the post that covers your province (for example Ottawa Embassy; Consulates General such as Toronto, Vancouver, Calgary — confirm current jurisdiction on [dfa.gov.ph](https://dfa.gov.ph/) / your post’s site).

## Forms and biometrics

COMELEC provides overseas voter forms (including **OVF1**) via its Overseas Voting pages and **iRehistro** online generation. Posts emphasize that generating a form **online is not the end of registration**: you generally must **appear in person** for biometrics and submission as instructed by the post.

Calgary PCG lists applications entertained in this period as including: registration; transfer of registration records; reactivation; change of name/correction of entries; change of address; inclusion/reinstatement; and certification.

**Links cited by posts:**
- COMELEC Overseas Voting: https://comelec.gov.ph/?r=OverseasVoting
- OVF forms (2028 NLE): https://comelec.gov.ph/?r=OverseasVoting/2028NLE_OV_RegForms
- iRehistro for overseas voters: https://irehistro.comelec.gov.ph/ovf1

## Documents commonly requested (Canada posts)

Bring originals and photocopies as your post lists. Examples from Vancouver PCG:

- **Work or study permit holders:** valid Philippine passport + current work or study permit
- **Permanent residents in Canada:** valid Philippine passport + PR card
- **Dual citizens:** Identification Certificate / Oath of Allegiance / Order of Approval from reacquisition of Philippine citizenship + secondary ID

Calgary PCG similarly lists a valid Philippine passport (or post certification if no valid passport), dual-citizen citizenship documents, or seafarer’s book where applicable.

Always use the **checklist on your post’s latest advisory**.

## Voting itself

Registration and **voting** are separate steps. Voting instructions (personal, postal, or other modes authorized for that election) are announced closer to the election by COMELEC and your post. Watch your post’s website and the COMELEC Overseas Voting Facebook page linked by Calgary PCG: https://www.facebook.com/overseasvotingph
