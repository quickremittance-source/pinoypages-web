# Benefits Filipino families often miss, and why filing matters

_From Pinoy Pages 2026–2027_

Many of the biggest payments Canadian families get **aren't applied for separately. They come from filing your tax return.** If you (or your spouse) skip a year because "wala naman akong income" or "maliit lang," you could be leaving money on the table. Here are four to know.

> **Name change:** In **July 2026**, the **GST/HST credit was renamed the Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit (CGEB)**. The CRA says eligibility and calculation stay the same, and there's a **25% increase** to the amount that runs for five years (2026 to 2031).

## 1. Canada Child Benefit (CCB)

A **tax-free monthly payment** for families raising children under 18.

- **July 2026 to June 2027** (based on your 2025 return), up to:
  - **$8,157 per year** ($679.75/month) for each child **under 6**
  - **$6,883 per year** ($573.58/month) for each child **aged 6 to 17**
- The full amount applies when **adjusted family net income is under $38,237**. Above that it's gradually reduced.
- Up to **$3,480** more per year for each child eligible for the **disability tax credit** (Child Disability Benefit).
- **For July 2027 to June 2028** (based on your 2026 return): ⚠️ _PLACEHOLDER. Not yet published by the CRA (amounts are indexed every July)._
- **How to apply:** CRA My Account (“Apply for child benefits”), by mail with **Form RC66**, or — for many newborns — the provincial/territorial **Automated Benefits Application** when registering the birth (do not also re-apply with RC66 if you used that service). Newcomers may also need **RC66SCH**. Details: [newcomer checklist](/magazine/newcomer-first-tax-return).

## 2. Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit (formerly the GST/HST credit)

A **tax-free quarterly payment** for low- and modest-income individuals and families, to help with groceries and day-to-day essentials. Related provincial or territorial amounts may be included.

- **July 2026 to June 2027**, up to:
  - **$679** if you're single
  - **$890** if you're married or common-law
  - **$234** for each child under 19
- Typically paid on the **5th** of **July, October, January and April** (or the last business day before the 5th if that day is a weekend or holiday). If the quarterly amount is under **$50**, the CRA may pay the whole year in July.
- You generally **do not apply every year** after the first time: **file a tax return**, and the CRA checks if you qualify. File even with little or no income; filing late can disrupt quarterly payments until the return is assessed.
- **For July 2027 to June 2028:** ⚠️ _PLACEHOLDER. Not yet published._

## 3. Ontario Trillium Benefit (OTB)

Combines three Ontario credits. **You apply by filing your return and completing the ON-BEN form** in your tax package, even if you have no income.

- **Ontario Energy and Property Tax Credit** (2026 benefit year, July 2026 to June 2027), up to **$1,307** (ages 18–64) or **$1,488** (65+). For people who **paid rent or property tax** on their main home in 2025, among other conditions.
- **Ontario Sales Tax Credit**, up to **$378** for you, plus up to **$378** for your spouse or partner and for each child under 19.
- **Northern Ontario Energy Credit** (for Northern Ontario residents), up to **$189** single / **$290** family.
- **For July 2027 to June 2028** (based on your 2026 return): ⚠️ _PLACEHOLDER. Not yet published by Ontario._

> 💡 **Renters, this one's for you:** many people don't know rent can count toward the Ontario Energy and Property Tax Credit. Keep your rent records and fill in ON-BEN.

## 4. Canada Workers Benefit (CWB)

A **refundable tax credit** for people who are **working and earning a low income**. You claim it on your tax return.

- Up to **$1,665** if single, up to **$2,869** for families, plus up to **$860** more if you're approved for the disability tax credit (CRA benefits infographic, updated July 2026). ⚠️ _Editor: confirm on the CRA "How much you can get" page that these are the 2026 tax-year amounts. That page still showed 2025 amounts on Oct 17, 2026._
- You may be able to get **up to 50%** of your CWB in advance (the Advanced CWB) in **July, October and January**.

## Why filing matters (even with no income)

- Benefits are **recalculated every July** based on **last year's** tax return.
- **Both spouses or partners must file** for family benefits to be calculated.
- **File by April 30** so July payments arrive on time. Filing late can **delay** them.
- **Newcomers** can apply before their first return. See our [newcomer checklist](/guides/newcomer-first-tax-return).

## Make sure you're not missing out

- [ ] Did both of you file last year?
- [ ] Is your marital status, address and direct deposit up to date with the CRA?
- [ ] Did you complete ON-BEN (rent or property tax)?
- [ ] New baby, or a child who moved in with you? Apply for the CCB.
- [ ] Modest income and a simple situation? A **free tax clinic** may be able to file for you.
- [ ] Use the **CRA child and family benefits calculator** to estimate your amounts.
