# 2027 tax season: the deadlines that matter (for your 2026 return)

In 2027 you'll file your tax return for the **2026 tax year** (January 1 to December 31, 2026). Here are the key dates, from the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA). We also keep them on our [tax deadlines page](https://pinoypages.ca/tax-deadlines) _[URL TBC]_.

## The two big dates

| Date                       | What's due                                              | Who it applies to                                                            |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Friday, April 30, 2027** | **File** your 2026 return **and pay** any balance owing | Most individuals                                                             |
| **Tuesday, June 15, 2027** | **File** your 2026 return                               | You, if you or your spouse/common-law partner were **self-employed** in 2026 |

> **Important for self-employed Pinoys:** June 15 is only the deadline to _file_. **Any tax you owe is still due April 30, 2027.** If you pay late, interest can start even if you file on time by June 15.

(Exception: if your 2026 business spending was mostly the cost of tax shelter investments, your filing deadline is April 30, 2027.)

## Other dates to put in your calendar

| Date                                       | What happens                                                 | Notes                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Thursday, December 31, 2026**            | End of the 2026 tax year                                     | Income and expenses up to this date go on your 2026 return.                                                                                    |
| **End of February 2027**                   | Employers file T4/T4A information returns                    | This is the deadline for employers to file T4 and T4A slips with the CRA. Look out for your slips around this time.                            |
| **Monday, March 1, 2027** ⚠️ _PLACEHOLDER_ | Last day for RRSP contributions to count on your 2026 return | **The CRA has not yet posted the 2026-year RRSP date.** (For the 2025 tax year it was March 2, 2026.) We'll confirm once canada.ca is updated. |
| **Monday, March 15, 2027**                 | 1st quarterly instalment for 2027                            | Only if the CRA asks you to pay by instalments (common for self-employed people).                                                              |
| **Tuesday, June 15, 2027**                 | 2nd instalment for 2027                                      | Same day as the self-employed filing deadline.                                                                                                 |
| **Wednesday, September 15, 2027**          | 3rd instalment for 2027                                      |                                                                                                                                                |
| **Wednesday, December 15, 2027**           | 4th instalment for 2027                                      |                                                                                                                                                |

## If you're registered for GST/HST

- **Monthly or quarterly filers:** file and pay **one month after** the end of each reporting period.
- **Annual filers:** usually file and pay **three months after** your fiscal year-end. But if you're **self-employed with a December 31 year-end**, your GST/HST **payment is due April 30, 2027** and your **filing deadline is June 15, 2027**.

## Weekend and holiday rule

When a CRA due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday or a public holiday the CRA recognizes, your return or payment counts as on time if the CRA receives it, or it's postmarked, on or before the next business day.

## Why filing on time matters even if you don't owe anything

Benefits like the **Canada Child Benefit** and the **Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit (formerly the GST/HST credit)** are calculated from your tax return. Both you **and** your spouse or partner need to file every year, even with little or no income. Filing late can delay your payments. See [Benefits Filipino families often miss](/guides/benefits-families-miss).

## Quick checklist

- [ ] Put April 30 and (if self-employed) June 15, 2027 in your phone calendar
- [ ] Self-employed? Put money aside to pay by **April 30**, even if you file in June
- [ ] Check whether the CRA has asked you to pay instalments
- [ ] Make sure your address and direct deposit are up to date with the CRA
