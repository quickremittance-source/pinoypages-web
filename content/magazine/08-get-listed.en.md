# Get listed free, or stand out with an upgrade

_Pinoy Pages 2026–2027: the Foundation Edition directory for Filipino-Canadian tax, accounting, insurance, banking and mortgage pros, independent professionals and small businesses in the GTA._

## Start with a free listing

Every qualifying business can be listed **free**: **business name and contact details**. No catch, and no obligation to upgrade. Your free listing stays free.

👉 [Claim or add your free listing](https://pinoypages.ca/list-free)

## Join the community and get verified

_Membership and verification is **$199 CAD for the Foundation Edition**, a six-month pilot limited to 100 memberships. No HST is charged. Future editions run for one year; ended editions are archived and remain available to view._

| Option       | Price                 | What you get                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------ | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Free**     | $0                    | Business name + contact details only. Unverified: no badge, no logo                                                                                                                 |
| **Membership and verification** | $199 CAD for the Foundation Edition | Six-month pilot, limited to 100 memberships. A featured listing with your logo and eligibility for the identity-only **Verified Kabayan** badge, earned on a ~15-minute live video check. |
| **Optional photo landing page** | Price on enquiry | A standard ad landing page with one photo. |

**Payment never buys the badge.** Verified listings are only _eligible_. The Verified Kabayan badge certifies **identity only** and is earned on a ~15-minute live video check (owner on camera, ID viewed and never stored, business licence or registration checked, a live premises walkthrough or screen-share, one proof of trading). Verification is online only.

**Keeping your badge.** The badge stays while your Verified listing is renewed each issue; a light re-check at renewal updates “Verified [date]”. Keep your phone and listing current. A dead phone, a closed business or a complaint pauses the badge; no renewal removes it.

The printed Foundation directory will be released after 100 memberships are confirmed and mailed to businesses. Print date and quantity are to be confirmed. Print ads are not being sold during the Foundation Edition.

**Website included free:** every listing also appears on the Pinoy Pages website at no extra cost.

## Good to know

- **Scan safely.** Every Pinoy Pages QR opens a page on the Pinoy Pages website. Check the preview says pinoypages.ca before you tap. Our pages never ask for a login, a payment or personal information.
- **No guarantees.** We don't promise calls, clients or sales. Reporting is not promised as part of the membership offer.
- **Paid placements are labelled.** Readers can always tell what's an upgrade.
- **What the badge means.** Verified Kabayan certifies identity only: the owner's identity and business licence or registration, checked on a live video call. It is not a recommendation of the quality of a service. Readers are encouraged to do their own checks.
- **Keep it accurate.** You're responsible for making sure your listing (licences, designations, services) is truthful and current.
