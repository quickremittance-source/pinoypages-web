## Good to know

- **Scan safely.** Every Pinoy Pages QR opens a page on the Pinoy Pages website. Check the preview says pinoypages.ca before you tap. Our pages never ask for a login, a payment or personal information.
- **No guarantees.** We don't promise calls, clients or sales. We **report your scan count every month** (Verified and above), so you can judge for yourself.
- **Paid placements are labelled.** Readers can always tell what's an upgrade.
- **What the badge means.** Verified Kabayan confirms three things only: kabayan-owned or run, really trading, and a real person behind the ZCard. It is not a recommendation of the quality of a service. Readers are encouraged to do their own checks.
- **Keep it accurate.** You're responsible for making sure your listing (licences, designations, services) is truthful and current.
