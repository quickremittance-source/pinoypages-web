# Business of the Week — editorial template

_From Pinoy Pages 2026–2027_

Use this checklist when profiling a **real** listed business. **Do not invent** owners, quotes, addresses, years in business, client stories or statistics. Every factual claim needs a source (owner interview on record, public registry, or the business’s own verified listing).

## Required story fields (fill with verified facts only)

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Business legal / trade name** | As on listing or business registry |
| **Owner or public spokesperson** | Full name only with consent; role/title |
| **City / neighbourhood** | Service area as stated by the business |
| **Category** | Directory category (tax, grocery, remittance, etc.) |
| **Year established** | Only if documented |
| **Contact** | Phone, email, website, Messenger — from listing |
| **Languages served** | e.g. English, Tagalog, Cebuano — as stated |
| **Hours** | As published; note holidays |
| **Products / services (3–5 bullets)** | No superlatives (“best in GTA”) unless quoting a sourced award |
| **Why kabayan choose them (owner’s words)** | Direct quote with date of interview; no paraphrased fake quotes |
| **Community tie-in** | Real sponsorships or associations, documented |
| **Listing CTA** | Link to `/listing/...` and claim/upgrade path if unpaid |
| **Photo credits** | Owner-supplied with written permission, or stock labelled as such |

## Structure (suggested headings)

1. Opening scene grounded in a **real**, consented detail  
2. Who they are and what they offer  
3. How to reach them (listing embed)  
4. One practical tip for customers (factual)  
5. How to get listed / claim your listing CTA  

## Tagalog pair

Publish a matching `*.tl.md` with the same facts. Mark `status` with **NEEDS NATIVE-SPEAKER REVIEW** until reviewed.

## Hard rules

- No fictional businesses, composite characters or invented testimonials  
- No unsourced rankings or “#1” claims  
- Paid features must be labelled  
- If the business declines an interview, do not publish a profile from secondary gossip
